// HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. That's the whole game.

1

Sign up free

Every middle schooler in Austin gets a free account. A parent signs you up, since you're under 18.

2

Learn and level up

You learn on an app called Timeback. Finish a topic, level up, keep climbing. You can even blow past your own grade.

3

Test, and win cash

You take the MAP test (the same one your school already gives), watched so nobody can cheat. Top scores win real money.

What you can win.

Three kinds of prizes. Two $100,000 crowns for the best mathematician and best reader in all of Austin. A $50,000 prize for whoever works the hardest. And $1,000 winners in every zip, so you don't have to beat the whole city.

// The one any kid can win

$50,000

The Effort Grand
