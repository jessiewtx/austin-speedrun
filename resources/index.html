FAQ
FAQ · GT School

Questions?
Answered.

The things families ask most about the Speedrun, in plain English. Still stuck? The parent page has a chatbot that can help.

How much does it cost?
Nothing. Entry is 100% free, and every registered kid gets full TimeBack access all season at no charge. Free entry is also what keeps this a lawful skill contest, not a sweepstakes.
Who can enter?
Any student in grades 6–8 during the 2026–27 school year who legally lives in the five-county Austin metro (Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, Caldwell), in any school type: public, private, charter, or homeschool. Eligibility is based on where you live, not where you go to school.
What can my kid win?
Over $340,000 in real cash: two $100,000 crowns (top math, top reader, which stack into a $200,000 Double Crown), a $50,000 effort grand plus a $5,000 prize each month, $10,000 to the best math and reader in every grade, and referral prizes. See the full breakdown.
How do the referral prizes work?
Refer friends and you're both rewarded. The top three referrers all season win $5,000 / $3,000 / $2,000, and there's a $1,000 raffle every month (each friend you refer is one entry, and you're both in the draw). Referrals only count once both of you finish the baseline test and 5 hours on TimeBack.
What are TimeBack and GT School?
TimeBack is GT School's mastery-based learning app: personalized lessons let kids learn core academics in about two hours a day, advancing only after they truly master each level. Every registered kid gets it free for the season. GT School (gt.school) is the school running the contest.
How much time does it take?
About two hours a day is a typical pace, but you set your own. A little every day keeps you climbing; kids chasing the biggest prizes put in more, especially on weekends.
How are winners decided, and how do you stop cheating?
Every big prize is decided by proctored scores. Kids take a proctored baseline and a proctored end-of-season test; anyone scoring above the 95th percentile for their age is invited to an in-person, proctored final in Austin that decides the $100K and $10K winners. Effort prizes count only TimeBack XP on mastered work, telemetry-audited before payout. Every cash winner verifies ID and residency (and completes a W-9) before a dollar is paid.
Can we still join if we hear about it late?
Yes, registration stays open all season and no prize depends on a baseline, so a late start loses nothing on the score boards. The monthly effort prizes and referral raffle give late joiners a fresh shot every month.
Are the prizes taxable?
Yes. Contest winnings are the child's income; prizes of $600 or more trigger a 1099 and a W-9 at claim, and the winner is responsible for any taxes owed. Families choose how a prize is paid: cash to a custodial account, a 529, a gt.school tuition credit, or a Trump Account. More on the parent page.
Is it safe for my child?
Yes, it's built parent-first. A parent registers first and gives verifiable consent (COPPA) before any info is collected from a child. Parents sign entry, releases, and prize acceptance. We collect only what's needed and never sell your data, and existing IEP/504 accommodations are honored on every test.
What language is the program in?
All content, registration, and communication is in English, consistent with TimeBack and the proctored assessment.
