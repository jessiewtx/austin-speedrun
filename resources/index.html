Questions

Frequently asked

How much does it cost?
Nothing. Entry is 100% free, and every registered child gets full, unlimited Timeback access for the entire season at no charge. Free entry is also what keeps this a lawful skill contest rather than a sweepstakes.
Who is eligible?
Every student in grades 6–8 during the 2026–27 school year who legally resides in the five-county Austin metro (Travis, Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, and Caldwell), regardless of school type: public, private, charter, microschool, or homeschool. Eligibility is based on where the student lives, not where they go to school.
My child is homeschooled. Can they compete?
Absolutely. Homeschoolers compete in their zip-code pool alongside every other kid in the neighborhood, and there's a virtual "Homeschool Austin" campus for school-level boards. The zip pool is the homeschoolers' home turf.
How much time does it take?
About 30 minutes a day is enough to compete casually. Kids chasing the biggest prizes will do more. The speedrun pace is roughly 30 hours to master a full subject at a grade level, especially on weekends and over winter break, which is where the standings really move.
How do you prevent cheating?
Every prize-bearing score comes from a proctored sitting only: AI proctoring on remote tests and in-person proctored finals for the largest prizes. And no prize is tied to a pre/post delta, so there's no baseline to tank: the Effort Grand counts only Timeback XP earned on mastered work, telemetry-audited before payout. We publish a methodology memo and invite audits.
Are the prizes taxable?
Yes. Contest winnings are the child's income; prizes of $600+ trigger a 1099 and a W-9 at claim, and the winner is responsible for taxes. Families choose how a prize is paid (cash, Trump account, 529, or tuition scholarship), and we'll publish a plain-English parent tax FAQ before the season starts.
What are Timeback and GT School?
Timeback is GT School's AI learning platform. It builds personalized lessons so kids can master academics in about 2 hours a day, and they advance only after true mastery. Every registered child gets full Timeback access free for the Speedrun season. GT School (gt.school) is the school running the contest.
What are the top prizes?
Two $100,000 subject crowns sit at the top: the Math/Science Crown (best young mathematician) and the Reading/Language Crown (best young reader), each for the highest proctored score in the metro. There's no overall composite prize, but the crowns stack: one child topping both boards wins the $200,000 Double Crown. The $50,000 Effort Grand (most Timeback XP, which counts only for mastered work at each kid's own level, so any kid can win it) is the third pillar, plus $10k grade champions, a $5,000 monthly effort prize, and three $1,000 winners in every zip.
Can we still join if we hear about it late?
Yes, registration stays open all season, with no growth-window cutoff because no prize depends on a baseline. A kid who joins in November loses nothing on the two subject-crown score boards, and the monthly effort prizes give late joiners a fresh shot to out-grind everyone, every single month.
What language is the program in?
All program content, registration, and communications are in English, consistent with the tools involved (Timeback and the proctored assessment).

        The standard

        How to read this page.

        A citation is only worth having if it supports the exact sentence next to it. Three rules follow from that, and they hold whether this page is long or short.

        Named sources, not summaries

        Every entry names its authors, where it was published, and when, and points at the original wherever one is publicly reachable. You should never have to take our word for what a source says.

        Omission is the default

        A claim shows up here only when every source beneath it is published. We would rather say nothing than attach a source that doesn't survive being read. Some sentences on this site will never appear here, and that's deliberate.

        It changes, and it's dated

        Entries carry the date they were last materially updated. If a source is superseded or withdrawn, the entries resting on it come down with it rather than being quietly left standing.

        The bigger mission

        Ordinary kids, measured honestly.

        The contest is only worth running if the results hold up afterwards. That's what this page is for: everything the campaign claims should be checkable by the people it's aimed at.
