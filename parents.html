Why we run this

A talent search for Austin's gifted kids, and yes, we're putting up real money.

Austin is the first city in a national search for academically gifted kids. Here's the honest version of what that means, and what's in it for your family, before you give us a thing.

What GT School is

gt.school is a new kind of school built on Timeback, its AI mastery platform where kids learn core academics in about two hours a day and advance only once they've truly mastered each level, never stuck waiting for the class, or bored at the top.

Why a $100,000 contest

A star quarterback gets a stadium; a brilliant 7th grader gets a sticker. Gifted kids are everywhere and invisible, one per classroom, spread across every zip. Real money is how we surface that talent from every neighborhood, not just the kids already on a gifted track.

What's in it for GT School

Straight answer: this is also how we grow. We're betting that once families see what their kid can do on Timeback, some will want more. But entry is 100% free, with no purchase and no obligation to ever enroll. Win the money, keep the free season, walk away, that's a complete, fair deal.

What your family gets

A free full season of Timeback (a real look at how the learning works), a proctored score that shows exactly where your child stands, a shot at life-changing prizes in your own zip and city-wide, and recognition for talent that usually goes unseen.

Why the results are real

Proctored, norm-referenced, and open to audit.

Our whole credibility rides on nobody being able to say these numbers were gamed. So every dollar of prize money is paid only on proctored test results.

Norm-referenced & independent

Scores come from a norm-referenced, growth-measuring assessment given under proctoring and benchmarked against national norms, so a parent can see exactly where their child stands.

Proctored, where it counts

All prize-bearing scores come from proctored sittings only: AI-proctored (camera, screen, response-time forensics) remote tests, plus in-person Saturday sittings and in-person proctored finals gating the largest prizes.

Un-gameable by design

No prize depends on a pre/post delta, so there's no baseline to tank. Score prizes pay only on the proctored assessment and in-person final, and Effort XP accrues only for mastered work, telemetry-audited before payout. A published methodology memo invites skeptics to audit.

Safety & privacy

Built parent-first, by design.

Because many sixth graders are under 13, the entire program is structured around the parent account, which is also how we satisfy federal consent law.

Verifiable parental consent (COPPA)

No personal information is collected from a child online until a parent completes verifiable consent. Parents register first; the child's account follows.

Parents sign everything

Minors can't be bound by contest rules, so a parent or guardian executes entry, any publicity release, and prize acceptance.

AI-proctored testing

Scored tests are proctored to keep results honest and the leaderboard fair for every family.

Accommodations honored

Existing IEP/504 accommodations (extended time, screen reader, large print) are honored on every proctored assessment.

Program language: English only
