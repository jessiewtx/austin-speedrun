Straight talk
for parents.

The student pages cover the game and the prizes. This page covers what you actually want to know: who runs this, why it's free, how your child stays safe, and how a prize reaches your kid.

The honest version

Why we're putting up real money.

Before you give us anything, here's the straight story, including the catch.

What GT School is

gt.school is a new kind of school built on TimeBack, its mastery-based platform. Kids learn core academics in about two hours a day and only advance once they've truly mastered each level.

Why a $100K contest

A star quarterback gets a stadium; a brilliant 7th grader gets a sticker. Gifted kids are everywhere and invisible. Real money is how we find that talent in every neighborhood.

The catch, honestly

This is also how GT School grows. But entry is 100% free, with no purchase and no obligation to ever enroll. Win the money, keep the free season, walk away, that's a complete, fair deal.

What your family gets

A free full season of TimeBack, a proctored score that shows exactly where your child stands, and a real shot at life-changing prizes, plus recognition for talent that usually goes unseen.

Safety & privacy

Built parent-first.

Because many 6th graders are under 13, everything runs through your parent account, which is also how we meet federal consent law.

Verifiable parental consent (COPPA)

No information is collected from a child until you complete verifiable consent. Parents register first; the child's account follows.

You sign everything

Minors can't be bound by contest rules, so a parent or guardian executes entry, any publicity release, and prize acceptance.

Only what's needed

We collect the minimum required to run the contest and verify winners, and we never sell your family's data.

Accommodations honored

Existing IEP/504 accommodations (extended time, screen reader, large print) are honored on every proctored assessment.

The payout

You choose how a prize is paid.

If your child wins, you pick from a menu built for a kid's future, not a video-game budget.

1

Cash

Paid into a UTMA / custodial account for your child. This is the default.

2

529 college savings

A tax-advantaged contribution that grows toward higher education. No annual plan limit.

3

gt.school tuition credit

Applied to tuition, often the highest effective value of the options.

4

Trump Account deposit

Fits smaller prizes cleanly, up to the annual contribution headroom.

A plain-English note on taxes: contest winnings are the child's income. Prizes of $600 or more trigger a 1099 and require a W-9 at claim, and the winner is responsible for any taxes owed. This is a free skill contest: winners are determined purely by proctored scores and verified mastery, with no random drawings, ever.

// Kept honest

Can the results be gamed? No.

Every big prize pays on proctored scores, benchmarked to national norms and telemetry-audited, no prize rides on a baseline your child could tank. It's one entry per student (verified by legal name and date of birth), at most 10 per household, and every cash winner re-verifies identity and residency before payout. The full format lives on the How it works page, with more answered in the FAQ.
