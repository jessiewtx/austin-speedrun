So what is the Austin Speedrun?

The plain-English version, no hype.

It's a free academic competition for every middle schooler in grades 6-8 across the Austin metro. GT School runs it to find, and pay, the city's most talented young students. You sign up free, spend the season learning on an app called TimeBack, and the highest scorers on Austin's leaderboard win real cash, including two $100,000 grand prizes.

// the app

Wait, what's TimeBack?

TimeBack is the learning app GT School students use. It's mastery-based: you can't move on until you actually get a topic, so you're always learning at exactly the right level, not too easy, not too hard. It tracks every minute of focused work as XP, which is how the effort prizes get decided. Most kids do about two hours a day, but you set your own pace.
